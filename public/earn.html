How Mining Works

The simple version — no jargon.

1

Hold the lightning button

Press and hold the ⚡ button for 2 seconds. That's it — your rig switches on and starts a mining cycle.

2

Let it run for 30 minutes

The ring around the rig fills up as time passes. You can close the app or lock your phone — the cycle keeps going in the background and picks up right where it left off when you come back.

3

Claim your Luck Tickets

Once the ring hits 100%, hit "Claim Tickets" to collect your reward. Then you can start a brand new cycle right away.

4

Upgrade for bigger rewards

Spending Gems💎 to upgrade your rig raises the range of Luck Tickets you can earn per cycle — the higher the level, the bigger the potential payout.
