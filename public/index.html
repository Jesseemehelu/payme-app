Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PAYME.

What is PAYME? +
PAYME is a modern rewards and referral platform where users can earn rewards by sharing their unique referral links.
How does the referral program work? +
Once you sign up, you receive a personal referral link. Share it with friends, and when they register, your referral is recorded.
How much do I earn per referral? +
You earn ₦15 for each eligible referral who registers using your link.
When can I withdraw my rewards? +
Reward withdrawals will be available directly from your wallet dashboard once withdrawal limits are reached.
Are there limits on referrals? +
There are no limits on how many friends you can invite to the platform.
What features are coming soon? +
We are developing interactive games, community challenges, and higher-tier rewards.
