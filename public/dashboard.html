How Mining Works

The simple version — no jargon.

1

Hold the lightning button

Press and hold the ⚡ button for 2 seconds. That's it — your rig switches on and starts a mining cycle.

2

Let it run for 2 minutes

Each cycle runs for 2 minutes. You can close the app or lock your phone — the server keeps the cycle running and your progress is restored when you return.

3

Claim your Luck Tickets

When the 2-minute cycle finishes, it counts as one successful cycle. Resume mining until you complete 10 successful cycles, then your Luck Tickets unlock for claiming.

4

Upgrade for bigger rewards

Invite verified friends to unlock higher miner levels. Level 2 starts at 20 verified referrals, and higher levels require more referrals and unlock larger Luck Ticket ranges.
