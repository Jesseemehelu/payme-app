✨ HOW TO PLAY

Lucky 3 — quick guide

A simple pick-3 number game. Here's all you need to know:

1
Pick 3 numbers

Tap any 3 numbers from 01–30. Tap a number again to remove it.

2
Choose a game mode

👑 Jackpot pays the biggest prize if all 3 numbers match. 💎 Cash Win pays if 2 or more match. ✨ Free Spins pays if just 1 matches — easiest to win.

3
Choose your entry

Pick how many Luck Tickets to spend. Bigger entries unlock bigger prizes — tickets are deducted before the draw.

4
Reveal your result

Tap "Reveal My Lucky 3". 3 numbers are drawn instantly — match them to your picks to win!
