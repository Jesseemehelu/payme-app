How to Play Tap Rush

Pick a stake, react fast, and tap accurately before the 20-second rush ends. Your score decides your reward.

  • 1Choose a stake: ₦200, ₦500 or ₦1000. This is deducted when the match starts.
  • 2Tap targets fast and accurately. Golden and mega targets are worth much more.
  • 3Score 5000+ to win 2× your stake in cash.
  • 4Score 3000+ to win 0.5× your stake in cash.
  • 5Score 1000+ to win free spins on the Spin Wheel (more spins for higher stakes).
  • 6Below 1000, the stake isn't returned — the targets get faster and smaller as the clock runs out, so top scores are rare.
  • 7Each week, the top 2 scorers win a bonus ₦1000 / ₦500 automatically.
