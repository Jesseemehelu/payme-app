How to Play Tap Rush

React quickly, tap accurately, and build your combo before the 20-second rush ends.

  • 1Tap normal targets as soon as they appear. Every successful hit increases your score.
  • 2Hit golden and special targets for bigger bonuses when they appear.
  • 3Keep hitting targets quickly to build your combo multiplier.
  • 4Avoid fake targets and missed targets — accuracy matters.
  • 5The match lasts 20 seconds. Your verified score is submitted when the timer ends.
  • 6Your latest completed game replaces your previous leaderboard score for this challenge.
